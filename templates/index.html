The process

From leaf to answer in four steps.

No lab, no sample mailer, no waiting for an extension office callback. The diagnosis happens on the phone already in your pocket.

01

Photograph the leaf

Take a photo of any leaf, stem, or fruit that looks off. Works with any smartphone camera, in the field, in daylight.

02

AI reads the pattern

The model compares lesion shape, color, and spread against a reference set of over two million verified field specimens.

03

Get a diagnosis

Within seconds you get a named disease, a confidence score, and how it's likely to progress across the rest of the plot.

04

Follow the treatment

A short, specific action plan — what to apply, how much, and when to check back — tailored to your crop and region.

Disease library

Built from real field specimens, not stock photos.

A running sample of what FieldScan currently recognizes. Every entry links to full symptom photos, progression stages, and regional treatment notes.

TOMATO

Late blight

Dark, water-soaked patches that expand fast in cool, wet weather. Can take a field in under a week.

CUCURBITS

Powdery mildew

A pale, flour-like coating across the top of the leaf. Slower moving, but it quietly cuts yield all season.

WHEAT

Leaf rust

Small orange-brown pustules scattered across the blade. Spreads on the wind between neighboring plots.

PEPPER

Bacterial spot

Small, dark, raised lesions with a yellow halo. Thrives in warm, humid, overhead-irrigated fields.
